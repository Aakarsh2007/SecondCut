These field counts are **not** total human actions or cognitive effort. Candidate discovery, model generation, approvals and payment processing are outside this subtask. No merchant-wide speed, ROI or human time-saving claim is supported.

The actor was not verified by the client timer. No trial was repeated to obtain a better result.

## Contrast evidence

`app/docs/CONTEXT_STUDY_RESULTS.json` records three actual Gemini calls: expensive cuts produced an uncut larger offer; a deadline conflict produced a later date with one width cut, plus a distinct discounted larger alternative; a material conflict produced no offer. All three expected development outcomes were observed. Cross-context terms replay with target-native citations rejects the incompatible offers for geometry, economic or material reasons. This supports context dependence in the sample, not universal model accuracy or worldwide novelty.

The earlier five-call quality study, including one timeout, remains available. A later successful study does not erase that failure. Real buyer liquidity, maker inspection, delivery, realized profit and customer adoption remain unverified.
