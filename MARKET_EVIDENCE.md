# SecondCut: public problem evidence and differentiation

Reviewed 28 October 2026. These are public sources, not interviews, independent verification of every anecdote, a demand estimate or a willingness-to-pay study. No person or business was contacted.

## Evidence of the problem

1. [WOODWEB: Managing a Customer Cancellation](https://woodweb.com/knowledge_base/Managing_a_Customer_Cancellation.html), dated 21 April 2011. The original poster describes a roughly $20,000 kitchen cancellation after cut sheets were produced and materials delivered, and later states a 50% deposit was taken. This supports the existence of stranded planning/material investment. It does not establish a cancellation frequency, current market size, legal refund entitlement or suitability of adapting cabinetry into a desk. Forum replies contain conflicting legal opinions; they are not used as legal guidance.
2. [Bespoke Wood Studio FAQ](https://www.bespokewood.studio/faqs-1). This maker's published process uses deposits to secure materials and describes a limited early-cancellation refund policy. Its custom work is produced for a particular client, with variable lead times. This directly supports modeling incurred materials, dates and explicit cancellation terms. One maker's policy cannot be generalized to other sellers or jurisdictions.
3. [Reddit woodworking coffee-table cancellation report](https://www.reddit.com/r/woodworking/comments/irw529). Search-index text describes a maker's customer declining a completed custom coffee table. Direct full-page retrieval failed, so this is supplementary self-reported evidence only; no detailed costs, outcome or demand figures are inferred from it.

## Related solutions already exist

Additional sources reviewed 29 October 2026: [Vermont Woods Studios](https://vermontwoodsstudios.com/policies/refund-policy) explains the difficulty of reselling customer-specific furniture and early investment in wood/scheduling. [9th Street Designer Clearance](https://www.9thstreetdesignerclearance.com/our-story) describes an established channel for cancelled special orders. These strengthen problem evidence while weakening any claim that cancelled-order resale itself is novel. Policies are not generalized or used as legal guidance. See COMPETITIVE_RESEARCH.md for the broader comparison and public hackathon-project review.

- [Unbuilt](https://unbuilt.co/) explicitly describes resale of surplus and cancelled-project stock. Reselling cancellation inventory is therefore not a new category, and SecondCut must not claim that no alternative exists.
- [Chairish cancellation support](https://support.chairish.com/hc/en-us/articles/44166765189137-Managing-Returns-Cancellations) discusses cancelled orders and associated seller costs. This is an adjacent resale/transaction context, not evidence that Chairish uses SecondCut's workflow.
- [Etsy personalized listing support](https://help.etsy.com/hc/en-us/articles/360000344528-How-to-Offer-Personalized-Listings) documents custom requirement capture. Merely collecting customization notes is not a defensible novelty claim.

## What this prototype specifically demonstrates

The implemented combination is a pre-completion raw-tabletop adaptation plan, source-cited AI extraction, deterministic physical/economic rejection, fixed exact-term multi-account review, separately verified replacement authorization/capture and original-buyer refund, durable signed-event handling and linked journey evidence. Its strongest comparison is a demonstrated non-exact dimensional match whose modification cost and refund path are explicitly evaluated.

The reviewed public pages do not prove that competitors lack similar private or emerging features. This research is not a patent, global novelty or hackathon-submission clearance search.

## Claims allowed in a pitch

- Public makers report custom-order cancellation problems after investment in work/materials.
- The prototype tests whether a specified adaptation is feasible and economically worthwhile under explicit assumptions.
- Provider integrations and approvals have separate technical evidence; the joined case must show every checkpoint before claiming an end-to-end success.

## Claims not established

No real replacement-buyer network, guaranteed buyer availability, actual manufacturer cost validation, willingness to pay, measured cancellation recovery rate, actual physical delivery, measured avoided waste, carbon savings, universal novelty or probability of winning has been established. The three test accounts are controlled by the builder; they are not three independent customers.
