# Private validation: close the evidence gaps

Keep code and the repository private during development. No outreach, messages, public listings or purchases are authorized by this document. A person reviewing an anonymized case need not see the code or full business plan.

Software can enforce a refusal, but it cannot create a willing customer, inspect timber or certify an unobserved quote. Use the evidence requirements below instead of calling every green software checkpoint commercial validation.

## Gate 1 — a permissioned cancellation record

Use one actual desk/table cancellation that the workshop has permission to discuss. Remove customer identity, addresses, account identifiers and payment secrets. Use a case label such as M1-C1. Record which details came from a maker, a document, a public source or an assumption.

| Required fact | Evidence/reference | Current value |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Production stage and actual stock dimensions | Maker measurement/date | Unknown |
| Material, finish, condition, joinery and base fit | Maker inspection/date | Unknown |
| Ownership and cancellation obligations | Workshop confirmation; unresolved legal questions stay unresolved | Unknown |
| Incurred materials/labour | Permissioned cost record | Unknown |
| Remaining completion and adaptation quote | Dated maker quote with expiry | Unknown |
| Payment/refund fees, tax and contingency | Merchant-specific calculation | Unknown |
| Available capacity and ready date | Workshop confirmation | Unknown |
| Best realistic alternative to adaptation | Supported resale/salvage/disposal quote, not invented zero | Unknown |

Pass only when necessary facts are supported. A public anecdote with no dimensions or costs establishes problem context, not a profitable recovery. Unavailable evidence means NEEDS EVIDENCE, not permission to fill in attractive numbers.

## Gate 2 — maker feasibility

Have the actual maker check the proposed dimensions and supported cuts, remaining stock, joinery, structural requirements, base compatibility, condition, tooling and capacity. Record the date, scope and any conditions of the review. Ask what would make the plan impossible. A non-maker clicking an acknowledgement is not this check.

Pass when the maker confirms a specific plan and dated quote. If they refuse, retain the refusal and its reason; do not redesign a physical product automatically. One maker's review does not validate every workshop or product.

## Gate 3 — a real opt-in buyer requirement

Use a genuine request from someone interested in a desk/table. Separately record required dimensions, material/finish, maximum budget, location, latest pickup, expiry and discovery permission. Share only what that person has permitted. Do not scrape contact details, contact people without authorization or treat social-media interest as a purchase commitment.

Show the actual proposed adaptation, total price and timing. Ask whether those specific terms meet the need, what would prevent acceptance and whether a modification is acceptable. Record acceptance, rejection or uncertainty without pressure. No real card or paid purchase is necessary for a preliminary requirement test.

Pass for preliminary buyer validation only when a real person confirms those specific terms; firm payment/contractual commitment is a later, separate gate. The software's owner-attested request fields do not independently verify this external interaction. Keep the record source honest.

## Gate 4 — protected economics

Use the production planner with stress protection enabled. Check the comparison against a supported cancellation alternative, not merely positive sale revenue. A suggested higher price is not a new buyer budget. A smaller refund is not allowed without a newly supported agreement and must never defer an unconditional obligation.

In the synthetic sample, USD 1,700 produces USD 100 margin but only USD 20 under the modeled overrun. Protecting the USD 100 minimum requires USD 1,780 under those assumptions. With a USD 1,700 ceiling, the protected decision is REJECTED. This is a useful refusal, not a failed demonstration. A genuine revised quote or accepted price needs a fresh plan and approvals.

The 20% scenario is one sensitivity assumption, not a probability or guarantee. Also inspect storage delay, extra labour, unquoted transport and failure of the alternative sale. Record unknown costs rather than claim risk has vanished.

## Gate 5 — financial linkage and provider failures

For the demonstrated sandbox path, keep original and replacement buyers distinct, approve exact current terms, independently verify capture/refund status and link signed events. Retain failed or uncertain operations. A refund can fail after a sale; PayPal operations are separate and are not an atomic escrow transaction.

Historical merchant deposits are a separate integration gap: this prototype collects a new sandbox original deposit per case. It must not silently attach an arbitrary historical capture. A production import would need merchant ownership, original payer/amount/currency, refundable balance, order linkage and duplicate-allocation verification before using that deposit. No such import is claimed implemented here.

## Gate 6 — physical completion

Actual maker readiness requires measured finished dimensions and the specified quality/structure checks. The replacement buyer separately confirms receipt. Report issues through the workflow. Do not record fictitious readiness or receipt to make a demo complete. The current joined test intentionally leaves handoff NOT_STARTED and closeout OPEN.

## Small pilot and decision

Start with one fully permissioned maker-reviewed case and one genuine buyer requirement. Before claiming a repeatable business, add different cancellation cases, include failures, and ask whether makers would actually use/pay for the service. This is exploratory evidence, not a statistically representative market study.

Track: supported feasible proposals, rejected/unavailable requests, quoted costs versus actuals, accepted price, verified payments/refunds, elapsed review time and participant-reported receipt. Do not infer carbon savings or avoided waste from tabletop area alone.

If a gate cannot pass, label the product a tested sandbox prototype with that remaining evidence gap. No interview, customer, cost quote or physical outcome should be invented.
